About us

Isiri Seeds, founded in 2020 by an industry expert with 25+ years of experience in export-oriented seed production, specializes i n high-quality F1 hybrid vegetable seeds for global markets. We offer reliable contract seed multiplication in cost-efficient, climate-suitable regions, using precise hand-pollination and modern production technology. With skilled growers, trained pollination teams, and experienced QA and processing staff, we ensure superior quality, consistent quantities, and timely delivery. Guided by our “Quality First” vision, Isiri Seeds blends India’s agricultural strengths with global standards, providing sustainable, dependable, long-term seed production partnerships worldwide.
